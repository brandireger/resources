Questions to chase

Comparison prompts

  • How do the French (Ribault, Laudonnière, Le Challeux) and Spanish (Fontaneda, Pareja, Oré) accounts of the Timucua differ?
  • Which texts were written by or with Indigenous people (e.g., the Hinachuba petition, Pareja's co-authored Timucua texts)?
  • Compare William Bartram's 1774 report with his 1791 Travels.
  • Who is the source: eyewitness, informant, or compiler? Record it in each item's notes.

Caveats

  • Years are as listed on the USF page; they may be publication or event dates, not both (for example, the Cabeza de Vaca joint report is filed under 1527).
  • Tiers (A/B/C) are a triage for researching Indigenous peoples and their relationships. They are not part of USF's catalog and can be wrong.
  • Contributors are the other names listed on each USF record. They look like editors, translators or student introducers; check the PDF.
  • Each text is an excerpt, mostly European-authored. Consider who is speaking and for whom.
  • Notes are stored in your browser only (localStorage). Use Export notes to back them up or move between devices.
